Accessibility

Checking…

ClipStack needs Accessibility access to send Cmd+V for you. Without it, an item is still placed on your clipboard so you can paste it yourself. If the switch is on in System Settings but this still says "not granted", the entry belongs to an older build: use Reset grant, then grant it again.

Shortcut

Click the field, then press the combination you want. It must include ⌘.
